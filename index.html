About videoadjust

Version __VA_APP_VERSION__

videoadjust is a private video editor that runs in your browser. Trim, crop and resize clips, correct colour or apply a style, add text, a watermark or music, balance and normalize the audio, then compress, convert, extract audio, make GIFs or save frames. Your videos are processed on this device and never uploaded.

Free to use, and it works offline after your first visit.

Created by Mark Bradley (BRADSEC).

Video processing is powered by the open-source Mediabunny library, with GIF encoding by gifenc. View third-party licences.
