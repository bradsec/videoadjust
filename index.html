About videoadjust

Version __VA_APP_VERSION__

videoadjust is a private video editor that runs in your browser. Trim, crop and resize clips, correct colour or apply a style, add text, a watermark or music, balance and normalize the audio, then compress, convert, extract audio, make GIFs or save frames. Your videos are processed on this device and never uploaded.

Free to use, and it works offline after your first visit.

Created by Mark Bradley (BRADSEC).

Video processing is powered by the open-source Mediabunny library, with GIF encoding by gifenc and icons from Lucide. View third-party licences.

Help

Choose a tool, trim your clip, adjust settings, then export.

Getting started

Open or drop a video. Everything runs in your browser, and your video never uploads.

Tools

Shrink video
Trim, adjust, and export an MP4 or WebM video.
Extract audio
Save audio as MP3, M4A, or WAV.
GIF
Make an animated GIF from the selected clip.

Trim

Drag the trim handles to set the kept range. Click or drag the ruler to move the playhead. Type exact Start and End times as seconds, m:ss, or h:mm:ss, with optional tenths. The frame buttons beside the playhead time step one frame. Set copies the playhead time to Start or End. Reset trim restores the full clip.

Use Cut sections to remove parts within the trim. Add cut starts at the playhead; edit its Start and End times or use Set, then remove a row if needed. Playback skips cuts, and Shrink, Extract audio and GIF export the joined parts. Cuts require exact encoding and change the kept duration shown in the summary. Reset all clears cuts.

Keyboard shortcuts

Space
Play or pause when focus is outside a control or editor.
, and .
Previous and next frame outside text fields.
Trim handles
Arrow keys move 0.1 seconds; Shift with arrows moves 1 second.
Playhead
Left and Right move 1 second; Shift with either moves 5 seconds. Page Up and Page Down move 10 seconds. Home and End jump to trim boundaries.
Time fields
Enter applies the time; Escape restores the current time.

Settings

Output
Choose format, size, quality, and other export options.
Fit to aspect
Add blurred, black, or solid colour bands around the cropped frame.
Audio
Include, mix, and adjust audio tracks.
Colour correction
Adjust exposure, contrast, saturation, and temperature.
Style
Apply a creative look.
Overlay
Add text or an image over the video.

Hover a setting for details.

Exporting and limits

Shrink shows an estimated output size before export. If the source is already small, Export makes a trimmed copy; choose Re-encode anyway to use adjustment controls.

Files above 2 GB show a warning. Desktop Chrome and Edge can save output straight to disk. On iOS Safari and Firefox, output stays in memory until you download or share it, so large exports may need more memory.

The app works offline after your first visit.
